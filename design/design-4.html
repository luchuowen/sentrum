Networks & Connectivity

Nobody notices the network — until it stops.

We design and install structured copper and fibre cabling, configure the switching, routing and VPNs that run on it, and cover the building with WiFi — then train your team and support it.

  • Structured cabling & fibre
  • WiFi
  • Servers

Remote & Satellite

It has to work where the fibre doesn't.

For field sites, camps and remote offices we install and commission VSAT, fabricate the mast and mounts, and add the UPS and solar power that keep the link up — for one site or a rollout across several countries.

  • VSAT & satellite
  • Masts & fabrication
  • Power, UPS & solar

Security Systems

It has to keep the wrong people out — on site and online.

IP door access control with readers, maglocks and fire-alarm integration; intrusion detection from PIR to vibration sensors; and firewalls, endpoint and mobile threat protection on the network itself.

  • Access control
  • Intrusion detection
  • Cyber security

Communications & AV

It has to let people meet, decide and respond.

IP telephony and video conferencing across offices; video walls for operations, situation and news rooms; audio-visual systems chosen after assessing the room and the people in it.

  • Unified communications
  • Video walls
  • Audio-visual systems

Power, Fabrication & Support

It has to stay powered, fit the site, and be looked after.

UPS-backed clean power, electrical fit-outs, lighting and solar. Masts, mounts and racks made on our own floor. Equipment supplied through major distributors — and the same engineers after go-live.

  • Power, UPS & electrical
  • Metal fabrication
  • IT equipment supply
  • Technical support

We survey the site, design the system and its bill of quantities, supply the equipment, install it with our own fabricated steel, commission and hand it over with training — and support it afterwards. One team, start to finish.
