Carousel controls — three directions

All three keep auto-advance, scroll stepping, swipe and keyboard. They differ only in how much control is shown. Each demo below is live.

A · Hairlinequietest

One thin line across the width. The blue segment is the current system and fills as it plays; earlier ones stay grey. Click anywhere on the line to jump. One word, "Pause", is the only button. No arrows, no counter.

B · Stretch dotsfamiliar

Six small dots. The current one stretches into a short bar that fills over 7 seconds. Tap a dot to jump; tap the active bar to pause or resume. Nothing else on screen.

C · Index ringmost informative
/ 06 Next:

One compact unit. The system number sits inside a ring that fills as it plays; tap the ring to pause. Two plain chevrons step back and forward, and "Next:" tells the visitor what's coming.
